One hydraulic topology has separate Sample and Rinse reservoirs entering selector V1, then a pump and a feed segment labelled 2.0 mL nominal. Selector V2 routes liquid either through one holder or along a bypass. Both routes end at one Waste reservoir. A seat sensor is located at the holder inlet. Below, P reads 4.0 mL bypass, then 8.0 mL holder, then sample. Q reads 8.0 mL holder, then 4.0 mL bypass, and is labelled diagnostic control. In both rinse phases of either protocol, V1 selects Rinse while V2 determines the destination.
