With flow stopped, the candidate replaces housing removal with sideways withdrawal of a self-contained cartridge. The housing and its two pipe interfaces stay in place, whereas the conventional sequence first detaches the entire housing from the pipe. To make this change possible, a single frame carries both filter layers through the side opening; the sealing ring remains on the housing. When seated, both layers lie between the inlet and outlet. The supplied geometric model checks that the layers leave with the frame in the same order and that the interfaces remain fixed. This teaching DEMO does not establish filtration performance or mechanical feasibility: no fluid simulation, seal test, manufacturing validation or performance measurement is available.
