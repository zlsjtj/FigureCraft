Teaching schematic. Two fixed cameras observe one passive checkerboard Board. Dashed undirected connectors denote observation associations, not optical rays or digital transmission. Each camera independently computes its own observed corner residuals and writes them to its corresponding record (solid arrows). The cameras do not communicate; records are not merged. Front-view symbols, connector bends, positions, and board pattern organize the explanation and do not specify optical geometry, distances, angles, or performance.
