Figure 1. Constructed DEMO of routing-limited rainwater storage. (a) Roof and tank silhouettes identify object categories; their geometry is schematic and does not encode capacity or water level. Exactly three roof branches feed two separate tanks. The single central supply splits into complementary fractions. Tank labels give initial volume / capacity; each tank has its own continuous load and spill drain. (b) The same three independently initialized schedules are compared over four minutes. Bars show total spill and identify the spilling tank in parentheses; the right column shows each tank's remaining capacity. Fixed tenth attains the 2 L bound from 72 L net addition and 70 L initial free space. All values are invented; no field measurements or forecast uncertainty are represented.
