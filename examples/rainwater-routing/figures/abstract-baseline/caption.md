Figure 1. Constructed DEMO of routing-limited rainwater storage. (a) Exactly three roof branches feed two tanks; only the single central branch is split. The diagram specifies connections, not physical geometry. Tank labels give initial volume / capacity. Loads withdraw continuously and spills leave by separate drains. (b) All three schedules independently start from the same initial state. Bars sum all four one-minute spill records for both tanks; W or E identifies the spilling tank. The right column retains each tank's unused capacity. Net addition exceeds initial free space by 2 L; fixed tenth attains this bound. No field data or forecast uncertainty is represented.
