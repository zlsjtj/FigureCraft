## Available comparisons and unavailable attribution

The four complete mechanisms provide explicit alternative controls. The different tab opening required by B is part of that alternative; identical wall stock does not mean identical tab geometry. The mass and part count can be compared across mechanisms. Different moving interfaces, dimensions, and locking elements mean that the comparison does not isolate one geometric change at a time.

The excluded qualitative D0 scratch failure motivated two simultaneous changes: clearance and underside opening. The dataset does not separately quantify the contribution of either change, and does not prove why two D gritty releases still fail. No claimed de-clogging rate, self-cleaning mechanism, particle trajectory, stress field, friction coefficient, or measured load-path partition is supplied.
