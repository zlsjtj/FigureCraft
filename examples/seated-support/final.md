# Three seated angles from one releasable stay

A display support can change its panel angle without changing its base or disconnecting either permanent pivot. In this synthetic teaching DEMO, one stay moves between three fixed grooves in the base. Adjustment depends on a simple distinction: the two permanent revolute joints remain assembled, while the operator supports the panel and lifts the stay's rounded foot before reseating it. The example establishes geometric configurations only; no prototype, experiment, or measured performance observation exists.

The three main rigid bodies are a rectangular base, a rectangular panel, and a narrow stay with an integral rounded foot. The base measures 140 × 80 × 8 mm and extends from x = −10 to 130 mm. The panel has a 120 mm hinge-to-top reference length, 64 mm width, and 3 mm nominal thickness. Its base hinge H and stay pivot P have parallel transverse axes, with P halfway along the panel. The 70 mm pivot-to-foot-centre stay has nominal width 6 mm and side thickness 2 mm. Its centre plane lies at y = −36 mm, beside the panel edge at y = −32 mm; a short axle stub provides the connection without passing the stay through the solid panel (Figure 1).

Three upward-open semicylindrical recesses have centres at d = 50, 75, and 100 mm behind H. Each has radius 2 mm and spans y = −40 to −32 mm. The integral foot has the same nominal radius and a transverse length of 6 mm. Seating places its centre Q at (d, 0) in the side projection, with one active unilateral contact. Equal nominal radii specify idealized geometry, not a manufacturing fit. With H = (0, 0), rearward x, upward z, and the base top at z = 0, the positive-height solution is

cos θ = (60² + d² − 70²)/(2 × 60 × d),

P = (60 cos θ, 60 sin θ), T = (120 cos θ, 120 sin θ).

For S1–S3, the resulting panel angles are 78.463041°, 61.278307°, and 43.531152°. The corresponding top-point coordinates T are (24.000000, 117.575508), (57.666667, 105.235714), and (87.000000, 82.649864) mm. Moving the seated foot rearward lowers the top and increases its horizontal projection; that projection is not occupied desk area, and the base dimensions remain fixed.

The fourth recorded case, R, is an externally held vertical panel with P = (0, 60) mm. A stay directed 30° below positive x places Q = (60.621778, 25) mm, so the foot's lowest point clears the base datum by 23 mm. R has zero groove contacts, retains both pivots, and is neither a fourth seated setting nor a complete adjustment trajectory. Local joint clearances are assumed. Friction, holding load, tipping, fatigue, wear, vibration, readability, and novelty remain unestablished.
