The display support described here has a rectangular base and a panel that can be raised from the front part of the base. We have called it a desktop display support because the panel itself can carry printed information, although no sheet, printing process, or extra payload is part of this example. The dimensions are a teaching construction, and no object was built. The small table below is calculated. In particular, an entry with a large vertical coordinate is not an observed result from a test.

There are three receiving grooves in the base, at distances 50, 75, and 100 mm behind the main hinge axis. These distances are to the centres of the rounded seats, rather than to an edge of a groove. A short stay is attached halfway up the panel. The stay has an integral rounded foot which can sit in one receiving groove. The foot is not pinned to the base. Both the panel hinge and the stay's upper pivot remain assembled during an adjustment, while the operator supports the panel and lifts the foot out before moving it to another groove. We have not calculated the operating force. The grooves do not travel when the angle is changed.

The panel is 120 mm from its lower hinge axis to its top reference point, and its nominal width is 64 mm. The stay's pivot-to-foot-centre distance is 70 mm. The panel-side pivot is 60 mm from the lower hinge. The stay is a narrow metal-strip-like part beside one side of the panel, which is useful to state because putting it through the middle of a solid panel would describe a different object. The base extends 10 mm forward and 130 mm rearward from the hinge. Its plan width is 80 mm. Pin diameters and local clearances are secondary to the axis geometry and have not been designed for production.

The support positions give top-point heights 117.575508, 105.235714, and 82.649864 mm. The associated horizontal top-point coordinates are 24.000000, 57.666667, and 87.000000 mm; the ordering is the same as the three groove distances above. The base dimensions are unchanged in all three entries. We also kept one released configuration in the record: the panel is vertical and held externally, and the stay foot centre is 25 mm above the base's top datum. This is not a fourth supported setting. The foot is clear of every receiving groove in that configuration.

Our coordinate origin is the main hinge in the side projection. Positive horizontal distance means rearward along the base, and positive height means upward. The upper stay pivot follows the panel as it rotates. There is only one stay and only one rounded foot, even though there are three places where that foot can be seated. Two permanent revolute connections are present throughout the four recorded cases. The model supplies the angles and coordinates from fixed link lengths, but does not include friction, strength, hinge play, wear, tipping, vibration, or how easily someone can read the panel.
