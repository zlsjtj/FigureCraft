# Frozen evaluation questions

These questions are fixed before generation. Review actual anonymous outputs before learning the condition.

Paper: What changes relative to the inherited collector? What is the reason a reader might adopt it, and what is the cost? Does the revision explain the two different duplicate problems and whole-record replacement without treating all components as inventions? Are first publication and final agreement distinguished? What do the bursty diagnostic and beyond-retention profile establish, and what do they not establish? Is the reading order useful for a reader new to the system? Which important work is more or less visible? Quote exact sentences when reporting improvement or regression. No invented reviewer acceptance, timing confidence or real data.

Figure: Identify each member and its removed half. Can the reader locate the central contact and infer whether the tops are flush? Does any view imply overlap, a blind pocket, extra physical members or unsupported force/material claims? Which reading operation does each composition make easier or harder? Judge the displayed 160 mm figure and caption separately; is the caption explaining the figure or correcting a misleading picture? Compare silhouette, hierarchy, labeling and visual finish without numerical taste scores.

For both: science is a gate, not a substitute for quality. Equal or worse is allowed. Do not infer skill success from a tidy file or a long report. This is independent model review, not human review. A task used to develop a change must not later be called holdout validation.
