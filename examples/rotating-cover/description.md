# DEMO — selective viewing by cover rotation

This DEMO replaces removal of an entire opaque cover with rotation of a retained annular cover to select the contacts visible for inspection. The disk and central shaft remain fixed; only the cover moves. Eight contacts occupy equally spaced positions on the disk. A single 90° sector opening initially reveals contacts 1 and 2. Rotating the cover 90° clockwise reveals contacts 3 and 4 while the other six remain occluded. The illustrated change is therefore selective geometric exposure without moving the contacts. This conclusion follows from the specified geometry and assembly definition only. No prototype, experiment, safety assessment, protection rating, or inspection-time measurement is available; manufacturing feasibility and practical benefit remain unestablished.

## Alternative text

Three aligned top views compare a removed-cover baseline with two states of a retained rotating cover. On the left, all eight numbered contacts are visible around a fixed central shaft. The middle view has a dark opaque annular cover with a top-right quarter opening showing only contacts 1 and 2. In the right view the opening has moved to the bottom-right quarter, showing only contacts 3 and 4. A curved clockwise arrow identifies cover motion. Contact positions and the central shaft remain fixed. All views depict the same device rather than separate sets of contacts.
