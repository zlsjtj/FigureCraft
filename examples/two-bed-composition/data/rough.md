# Bench module write-up, unstructured draft — constructed DEMO

We tested four versions of a liquid treatment module. There were two rates and six fresh installations per version at each rate. We measured breakthrough at a fixed treated volume and the pressure drop. The new module has two removable mesh trays, silicone seals, a removable lid and guides. The trays were changed with a hand tool. The task used the same granule lots and bed masses in the versions. There are tables for the first operating period and service. There are photographs in the original notes only as descriptions; no photographs are provided here.

The inherited two-bed process and the fixed enclosure work. The new device has low pressure drop in several runs. A removable design is convenient. The configuration with a lid gasket is good at low flow but is not good at high flow. The retained version can be serviced faster than the original. We should report the numerical results and insertion tests. The time reported is the time for replacing the beds after draining, not the complete interruption to treatment. No adsorption kinetics were fitted. We did not measure the distribution of flow inside the grains. The two sorbents are not a new material. There is no claim of durability from this trial.
