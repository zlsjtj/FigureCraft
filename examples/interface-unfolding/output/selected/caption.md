Figure 1. Constructed DEMO of one G interface. The inset preserves the four-patch topology and highlights the W0–W1 interface; the larger view follows copied values from W1 to W0, with the W0 east-edge detail rotated 180°. Blue cells denote edge values or their copies; the gold strip is the receiving boundary. Cell counts are schematic. W0 requires (e, 17), where e is the partition epoch. It selects a ready matching slot, uses the ghost copy for boundary update 17, then acknowledges release of that occupied slot. The separate queued tag (e, 16) fails the same match check; its subsequent queue policy is unspecified. W1 may prepare step 18 and write the free slot when ready. If both slots remain live, W1 waits. Blue arrows show copying and use, the purple dashed arrow acknowledges release from the used boundary, and the grey dashed arrow shows a future write.
