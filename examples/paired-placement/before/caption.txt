The existing library registers A against its positioned overlap. A score of at least 0.8 selects that estimate; otherwise, the stage-grid translation is stored. The single immutable T5 record supplies both resamplers and their masks, through the undirected reference links; arrows show image processing or selection into the record. The plus signs denote illustrative paired fiducials. In the coordinate example, t = (84, 3) pixels maps p = (10, 18) to (94, 21) in both output planes. Faint tiles provide mosaic context, not a complete acquisition grid. Different stains are not treated as identical pixel maps. Shared placement does not remove a residual B-only optical shift or guarantee correct global placement after fallback.
