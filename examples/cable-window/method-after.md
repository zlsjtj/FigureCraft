In this original DEMO, only the outer sheath is opened locally, revealing the cable's internal layout while leaving both individual insulation layers and copper conductors uncut (Fig. 1). The two copper conductors lie side by side, each within its own polymer insulation layer; a common outer sheath surrounds the pair. The end-section representation shows the same cable and reveals the nested layers there, not at the sheath window. No shield, twisting, third core, load or current direction is represented. Dimensions, shape and colors are schematic. No leakage, thermal, safety, inspection-efficiency or other performance tests were performed, and no research novelty is claimed.
